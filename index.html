Product

What Tickster does

Tickster helps owners scan dogs for ticks using a guided mobile flow designed around close-range coat checks. It supports repeatable scanning habits, calm scan sessions, and better owner preparation before each pass.

Guided close-range scans

Tickster encourages slow close-range passes and practical capture distance instead of rushed full-body sweeps.

Coat and fur preparation

The app reinforces best practices such as good light, gently parted fur, and checking common hiding zones like ears, collar line, belly, armpits, and tail base.

Owner coaching

Tickster helps owners create a steady routine by using a simple scan cue, short first sessions, and reward-based repetition.

Tickster Pet Scan Routine guidance

How to prepare your pet, your phone, and the people doing the scan

01

Prepare your pet to scan

Prefer scanning when your dog is sleeping or relaxed. Calm timing makes it easier to part fur gently, reduce motion, and keep the process low stress.

02

Train owners and pets together

Use one simple cue like “Scan,” keep the first checks very short, reward right away, and lengthen the routine slowly as your dog gets more comfortable.

03

Use practical scan behavior

Hold the phone about 3–5 inches from the coat, move slowly, use good light, and check the zones where ticks most often hide.

FAQ

Tickster Pet Scan FAQ

Tickster Pet Scan helps dog owners perform routine visual tick checks, record scan findings, and maintain a care history for each dog. It is a screening and record-keeping tool—not a veterinary diagnosis.

Where can I download Tickster Pet Scan?

Tickster Pet Scan is currently in review with both the Apple App Store and Google Play. Once approved, it will be available for iPhone through the Apple App Store and for Android phones through Google Play. You will then be able to download the app from your device’s official app store, create a Tickster account, and add a dog profile to begin.

What does Tickster Pet Scan do?

Tickster guides you through a visual scan of your dog’s coat using your phone’s camera. It highlights possible tick candidates, records confidence information, and saves each completed scan to the selected dog’s Care Timeline. Tickster does not diagnose disease, confirm that a finding is a tick, recommend treatment, or replace veterinary care. Contact your veterinarian if you find an embedded tick, your dog seems unwell, or you have health concerns.

How close should I hold my phone when scanning my dog?

Hold the phone about 3–5 inches from the coat. Move slowly and use good light.

When is the best time to scan my dog?

Scan when your dog is sleeping or relaxed.

Does Tickster send camera images to an LLM or third-party AI service?

No. Tickster performs its visual scan analysis on the phone using local models and does not send camera images to an LLM API for analysis. Snapshot saving is optional and off by default. If you enable it, selected scan snapshots can be stored with your Tickster account so you can review them later or share useful context with your veterinarian.

Can I scan my dog without an internet connection?

Yes. Once you are signed in and your membership access is active, the core camera scan and local visual analysis can operate without a continuous internet connection. Internet connectivity is required for account access, subscription verification, cloud synchronization, optional snapshot uploads, and online weather or local tick-awareness information. Pending information can synchronize when a supported connection becomes available.

What Tickster subscription plans are available?

Tickster offers Monthly and Annual auto-renewable membership plans on both the Apple App Store and Google Play. Both plans provide the same Tickster membership features; only the billing period and store-displayed price differ. Eligibility, introductory offers, renewal terms, billing management, and cancellation options are presented and managed by Apple or Google before you confirm a purchase.

How does Tickster handle scan records, snapshots, and location?

Completed scan records are associated with your Tickster account and the dog you selected. Saving snapshot images is optional and off by default; you can enable it in Settings if you want visual context for later review or veterinarian discussions. Location permission is also optional and foreground-only, and is used to add local tick-awareness and weather context. Tickster does not use background location.
