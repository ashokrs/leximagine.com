Tickster Is A Screening Aid, Not Veterinary Care

Tickster is a routine screening and record-keeping aid. It guides a visual scan of your dog’s coat using your phone’s camera, highlights possible tick candidates, records confidence information, and saves each completed scan to the selected dog’s Care Timeline.

Tickster does not diagnose disease, confirm that a finding is a tick, recommend or provide treatment, or replace veterinary care. Do not rely on Tickster for medical decisions about your pet. Scan results may be incomplete or incorrect, and a scan that finds nothing does not mean your dog is free of ticks. If you find an embedded tick, if your dog seems unwell, or if you have any health concern, contact a licensed veterinarian.
