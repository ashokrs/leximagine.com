Immersive Shopping

Turn a grid of products into a place people want to explore.

Most online stores look the same: rows of thumbnails, a filter bar, and a lot of scrolling. LexImagine Immersive Shopping turns that grid into a space shoppers move through, so products are discovered the way they would be in a real shop. It all runs in an ordinary web browser: no headset, no special hardware.

About

The grid was built for catalogs, not for shopping.

A grid is efficient for searching but poor at showing what makes a product special: its scale, its craft, and the setting it belongs in. Immersive Shopping puts products back in context.

For venues and events

Let visitors browse a large physical space before they arrive.

For individual sellers

How it works

Built on the open web, so it works everywhere

01

Pure web front end

Every space is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, so it opens in any modern browser on a phone or computer. Nothing to install.

02

Immersive in 2D

The sense of moving through a space comes from the interface itself, not from VR hardware. Shoppers explore with the screen they already have.

03

Backed by real services

A supporting middleware and backend layer serves the products and content behind each space, so it behaves like a working store, not a demo.

FAQ

Immersive Shopping FAQ

What is Immersive Shopping?

Immersive Shopping replaces the usual grid of product thumbnails with a space shoppers can explore, so products are seen in context rather than as a list.

Who is it for?

Merchants who want more from a traditional storefront, venues that want visitors to browse a location, and individual sellers with a few special pieces to present.

Can I turn my existing online store into an immersive one?

Yes. Sundaram Pottery is an example: a traditional selling website transformed into an immersive shopping experience.

Can I share it on Instagram and other social media?

Yes. Immersive shops can be linked from Linktree, Instagram, and other social profiles, and some projects produce outputs made for sharing.

Do shoppers need a VR headset?

No. Immersive Shopping runs in 2D in an ordinary web browser. Shoppers get the immersive experience without a headset or any special hardware.

How does pricing work?

vaangungo.app is pay as you go. Merchant and venue projects are scoped individually; get in touch for a quote.
